# Cooling Incidents

## CRAC unit failure
1. Acknowledge the alarm in the BMS and identify the failed unit and its zone.
2. Check the hot aisle temperatures in the affected zone on the DCIM dashboard.
3. Confirm the redundant CRAC units in the zone ramped up fan speed automatically.
4. If any cold aisle inlet exceeds 27 C, open the floor tiles nearest the hot spot and deploy portable cooling.
5. Call the HVAC contractor under the maintenance contract and open a P2 incident.
6. If inlet temperatures exceed 32 C, notify customers in the zone and begin a controlled shutdown of non-critical racks.

## Chilled water leak detected
1. Identify the leak detection zone from the BMS alarm.
2. Send a technician to visually confirm; do not assume a false alarm.
3. If water is near power distribution, de-energize only after the electrical on-call engineer approves.
4. Close the isolation valves for the affected loop using the valve map in the facilities binder.
5. Place absorbent socks and log the incident with photos.
