# Juniper Pharma

## Overview
Juniper Pharma is a specialty pharmaceutical distributor serving 3,400 independent pharmacies from 4 regional DCs.

## Financials
Revenue was $1.1B with thin margins typical of distribution. Inventory is $95M.

## Recent news
- Expanding cold-chain capacity for biologics at its Memphis DC.
- Recently passed a DSCSA serialization audit.

## Operations
Demand is driven by prescriptions and is relatively stable. 28,000 SKUs. Replenishment is handled in the ERP with min/max levels reviewed annually.
