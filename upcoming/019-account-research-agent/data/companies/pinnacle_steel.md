# Pinnacle Steel

## Overview
Pinnacle Steel is a mid-size steel service center operating 9 processing plants in Texas and Oklahoma, selling cut-to-length and coil products to fabricators.

## Financials
Revenue was $780M. Inventory of $190M is mostly coil, valued at commodity prices.

## Recent news
- Completed an acquisition of a two-plant processor in Tulsa.
- Investing in a new laser cutting line.

## Operations
Purchasing is done per plant by buyers who follow mill lead times of 6-10 weeks. Few SKUs (about 1,800) but high value per unit.
