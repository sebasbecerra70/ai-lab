# Harbor Retail

## Overview
Harbor Retail is a specialty home and outdoor retailer with 212 stores across the US Southeast and Midwest, plus an e-commerce channel that ships from stores. Headquarters are in Charlotte, North Carolina.

## Financials
Fiscal revenue was $2.4B, up 9% year over year. Inventory on the balance sheet was $610M, and inventory turns fell from 4.1 to 3.6. Gross margin declined 140 basis points, which management attributed to markdowns on overstocked seasonal goods.

## Recent news
- Announced a new 600,000 sq ft distribution center in Columbus, Ohio, opening next spring to serve 70 Midwest stores.
- On the last earnings call the CFO said stockouts on top sellers cost an estimated $45M in lost sales while seasonal overstock drove markdowns.
- Selected SAP S/4HANA to replace a legacy merchandising system; migration is in phase one.

## Operations
Replenishment is run by a central team of 14 planners using spreadsheets on top of the legacy system. Store transfers are manual and handled by district managers. Ship-from-store orders are 22% of e-commerce volume.
