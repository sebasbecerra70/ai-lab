# Remote Work

Most roles can work remotely, either fully or in a hybrid pattern. Data center technicians and facilities staff are site-based and are not covered by this policy.

## Eligibility

Employees are eligible after completing 90 days in their role and a successful probation review. Managers can approve a fully remote arrangement for up to 12 months at a time, after which it is reviewed.

## Working hours

Remote employees are expected to overlap with their team for at least 4 hours a day between 09:00 and 17:00 in the team's main time zone. Calendars must show working hours and planned time off.

## Working abroad

Working from another country is allowed for up to 30 days in any 12 month period, with approval from HR at least 3 weeks before travel. Longer stays create tax and employment law obligations and are normally declined.

## Home office

The company provides a laptop and a one-time setup allowance of 500 dollars for a desk and chair. Internet costs are the employee's responsibility, except for on-call roles.

## Security at home

Work must be done on company devices only. Screens must lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Public Wi-Fi is allowed only with the company VPN connected.
