# Vendor Management

This policy covers buying software and services from third parties, from a 20 dollar SaaS tool to a multi-year data center contract.

## Purchasing thresholds

### Under 5000 dollars
Can be bought on a corporate card with manager approval. The tool must still be registered in the vendor inventory within 30 days.

### 5000 to 50000 dollars
Requires a purchase order, a quote from at least two vendors, and approval from the department director. Procurement aims to turn these around in 10 business days.

### Over 50000 dollars
Requires a competitive bid with at least three vendors, a security review and CFO approval. Multi-year commitments also need sign-off from legal.

## Security review

Any vendor that will process customer data must complete the security questionnaire and provide a current SOC 2 Type II report or ISO 27001 certificate. Reviews are valid for 12 months. High risk vendors are reassessed every 6 months.

## Renewals

The vendor inventory sends the business owner a reminder 90 days before an auto-renewal date. Contracts that are not reviewed by then are flagged to finance. Usage is checked at renewal, and seats that were unused for 60 days are removed before renewing.

## Offboarding vendors

When a contract ends, the business owner confirms that the vendor deleted our data and provides the deletion certificate within 30 days. Integrations and API keys for that vendor are revoked on the last day of the contract.
