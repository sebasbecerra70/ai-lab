# Expense Policy

Employees are reimbursed for reasonable business expenses that are submitted with receipts through the expense tool. Expenses must be submitted within 45 days of the purchase date. Late submissions require written approval from a director.

## Travel

### Flights
Economy class is required under 6 hours of flying time. Premium economy is allowed for 6 hours or more. Business class requires approval from the CFO before booking. Book at least 14 days in advance when the trip is known.

### Hotels
The nightly cap is 250 dollars in tier one cities such as New York, London and San Francisco, and 180 dollars everywhere else. Stays above the cap need manager approval in the expense tool before the trip.

### Meals
The daily allowance while traveling is 75 dollars, including tips. Alcohol is not reimbursed unless it is part of a customer dinner approved in advance. Team dinners are capped at 60 dollars per person.

## Equipment

Laptops are issued by IT and are not purchased through expenses. Employees may expense a monitor, keyboard or headset up to a combined 400 dollars per year for a home office. Mobile phone plans are reimbursed up to 50 dollars per month for roles that are on call.

## Training

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1500 dollars for courses, books and conferences. Conference travel is paid from the travel budget, not the learning budget. Certifications that the role requires, such as cloud provider certifications for platform engineers, are paid by the team and do not use the learning budget.

## Corporate cards

Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel more than four times a year. Personal purchases on a corporate card must be repaid within 15 days. Misuse of a corporate card results in the card being cancelled.
