# Receiving SOP

## Frozen and refrigerated loads
Frozen loads must be received at 0°F (-18°C) or colder. Refrigerated loads must be between 33°F and 40°F. Probe three cases from the rear, middle and nose of the trailer and record readings in the WMS.

## Damaged inbound pallets
Photograph the damage before unloading further, note it on the bill of lading before the driver signs, and move the pallet to the OS&D (over, short and damaged) area. Notify the vendor within 24 hours.

## Appointment no-shows
Carriers more than 2 hours late lose their appointment and must be rescheduled through the dock scheduling portal.
