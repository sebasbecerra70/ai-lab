# Shipping SOP

## Load sequencing
Load stops in reverse delivery order so the first stop's freight is nearest the trailer door. Heavy pallets go on the floor, never stacked above lighter product.

## Seal verification
Record the trailer seal number on the manifest and have the driver confirm it before departure. A missing or broken seal must be escalated to the shift supervisor.

## Hazmat
Hazardous materials require placards matching the hazard class and a shipping paper within the driver's reach.
