# Contoso Retail - TMS and visibility proposal

## Q: Do you have SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 certification?
We hold a SOC 2 Type II report covering security, availability and confidentiality, renewed annually, and ISO 27001 certification for our hosting operations. Reports are shared under NDA.

## Q: Describe your disaster recovery capabilities.
Production runs active-passive across two cloud regions. Our recovery point objective (RPO) is 15 minutes and recovery time objective (RTO) is 4 hours. Disaster recovery failover is tested twice a year and results are shared with customers on request.

## Q: What support tiers and response times do you offer?
Standard support covers business hours with a 4-hour first response for priority-1 issues. Premium support is 24x7 with a 1-hour first response for priority-1 issues and a named technical account manager.

## Q: How long do you retain customer data after contract termination?
Customer data is retained for 30 days after termination so it can be exported, then permanently deleted within a further 30 days. A certificate of deletion is provided on request.
