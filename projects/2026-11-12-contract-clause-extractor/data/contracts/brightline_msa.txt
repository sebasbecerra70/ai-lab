MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT between Brightline Systems Integration Corp. ("Provider") and Our Company ("Client")

1. Services. Provider will perform implementation services described in each Statement of Work.

2. Payment. Client shall pay undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt.

3. Term. This Agreement has an initial term of two (2) years and renews only by mutual written agreement of the parties.

4. Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on sixty (60) days written notice. Either party may terminate immediately if the other party becomes insolvent.

5. Limitation of Liability. Except for breaches of confidentiality and indemnification obligations, each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

6. Mutual Indemnification. Each party shall indemnify the other against third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage caused by its negligence.

7. Confidentiality. The parties shall keep Confidential Information secret during the term and for three (3) years thereafter.

8. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.
