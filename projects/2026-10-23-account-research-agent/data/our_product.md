# Stockwise

## Offering
Stockwise is inventory optimization software for multi-site retailers and distributors. It forecasts demand per SKU and location, sets reorder points and safety stock, and recommends transfers between stores and DCs.

## Capabilities
- forecasting: SKU x location demand forecasts with promotions and seasonality; typical forecast error reduction 20-30%
- replenishment: automated reorder points and safety stock; customers cut stockouts 25-40%
- rebalancing: store-to-store and DC-to-store transfer suggestions that free working capital
- integrations: prebuilt connectors for SAP, NetSuite, Manhattan and Blue Yonder WMS

## Ideal customer
Retailers or distributors with 50+ locations, 10k+ SKUs, inventory above $50M, and an ERP or WMS migration or a new DC in flight.
