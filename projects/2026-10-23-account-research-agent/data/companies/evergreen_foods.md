# Evergreen Foods

## Overview
Evergreen Foods runs 38 natural grocery stores in the Pacific Northwest.

## Financials
Revenue was $520M. Shrink on perishables is 4.8% of sales.

## Recent news
- Opened three new stores in Portland.

## Operations
Store managers place most orders directly with local suppliers. Central buying covers dry goods only.
